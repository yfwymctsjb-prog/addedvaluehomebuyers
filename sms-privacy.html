Internal SMS Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Internal Lead Alerts program. It describes this internal messaging program, not every activity of the business or every service used on the public website.

Program information

The program uses the designated recipient's mobile number and authorization record to deliver internal lead notifications. It also processes the limited seller-submitted information included in each notification, such as property address, seller name, callback number, asking price, condition and selling timeline. Messaging service providers may generate delivery status, timestamps, opt-out records and technical logs.

How information is used

This information is used to operate the internal notification program, deliver messages, handle help and opt-out requests, troubleshoot delivery and address abuse or legal obligations. Only information needed for the operational alert should be included.

Twilio and telecommunications providers process the information needed to transmit messages. Other service providers may process information only as needed to operate the notification system. Consent records and recipient routing details must not be placed in public website code or public verification documents.

No marketing or promotional sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. For marketing or promotional sharing, all the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties. This restriction does not prevent the limited service-provider processing needed to deliver messages and operate the program.

Retention and access

The business should retain program information only for operational, consent-documentation or legal needs and protect access to it. No fixed deletion schedule or specific security certification is promised by this policy.

Choices and contact

The enrolled recipient can stop operational SMS alerts by replying STOP and can request help by replying HELP. Questions about this program or requests concerning program information can also be directed to the public business contact at (281) 504-7854.

SMS is not encrypted end to end. The business should avoid including financial account details, credentials, government identification numbers or similarly sensitive information in alerts.
