Added Value Investments Internal Lead Alerts

This program sends operational notifications to the business owner's designated mobile number when a seller submits an inquiry through addedvaluehomebuyers.com. Messages may include a property address, seller callback information, asking price, condition and selling timeline so the business owner can follow up.

This program is for the enrolled business owner. Submitting the public seller intake form does not subscribe a seller to this SMS program. These terms do not authorize marketing texts to sellers or other third parties.

Optional participation

Participation is optional. Enrollment requires the recipient's explicit authorization during the private setup process and verification of the designated recipient number. Message frequency varies with website inquiries. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and get help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or call the public business contact at (281) 504-7854. After an opt-out, no further operational alerts should be sent unless the recipient explicitly re-enrolls; any permitted opt-out confirmation is separate from operational alerts.

Delivery and sensitive information

SMS delivery is not guaranteed. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. SMS is not an encrypted communication channel, so do not send passwords, payment information, Social Security numbers or similarly sensitive information through this program.

Privacy

For information about the program's handling of data, review the Internal SMS Privacy Policy.
