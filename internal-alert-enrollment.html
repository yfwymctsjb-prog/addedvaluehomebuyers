Internal Alert Enrollment Workflow

This is a description of the staff-only enrollment process, not a public enrollment form. It contains no private recipient phone number, notification email, EIN, personal address, signature or private consent record.

Review

The business owner reviews the internal-alert purpose, variable frequency, message-and-data-rate disclosure, STOP/HELP instructions, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Explicit authorization

The owner explicitly approves enrollment through the authenticated setup interface. Completing the seller intake form does not constitute enrollment.

Number verification

The designated recipient number is verified through Twilio before live alerts are enabled.

Record

The authorization and verification record is retained privately. Public carrier-verification materials describe the workflow without publishing the recipient's identity or routing details.

Scope

Enrollment is limited to the business owner's designated number. It does not authorize SMS to sellers, purchased lists or other third-party recipients.

Withdrawal

The recipient may reply STOP to end operational alerts. Re-enrollment requires a new affirmative authorization.
